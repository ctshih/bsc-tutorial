Building a Knowledge Base with an AI Agent

Every episode is the record of one real working session — each prompt, how the Agent reasoned and acted, and what the screen looked like, including the places where things failed.

About this edition

These are the English editions of all five episodes. The sessions they record were conducted in Traditional Chinese, so the screenshots are in Chinese and are shown exactly as captured — each one has an English transcript underneath, laid out the way the screen was. Prompts appear in English with the original wording beneath them.

The knowledge base built in these episodes also exists in English, as Wiki_Eng/: eleven paper entries, nine topic pages, an index, a changelog and a processing registry. It is worth opening alongside episodes 3, 4 and 5 — episode 4 builds a course out of one of its topic pages, and episode 5 uses one of its entries as a specification.

Suggested order

  1. Watch the video first for the shape of the episode — ten to thirteen minutes.
  2. Then read the page — the video carries the backbone; the page has every prompt and what the Agent actually did.
  3. Take the quiz. Seeing the reason at the moment you answer is worth far more than a score at the end.
  4. Do the exercise at the end of the page with your own sources. The course is designed to make you make the decisions.
